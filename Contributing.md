# Contributing to awesome-ai-research

Thank you for helping make this list better! Please take a minute to read the guidelines below before submitting.

---

## What belongs here

- Resources that are **genuinely useful** for someone preparing for or doing AI research
- Resources that are **free or have a meaningful free tier** (paid resources are fine if they're widely recognized)
- Resources with a **stable URL** (no personal blog posts, no broken links)

## What doesn't belong here

- Purely promotional content or sponsored resources
- Resources that duplicate something already listed
- General programming tutorials not specific to AI/ML/research

---

## How to contribute

1. **Fork** this repository
2. **Add your resource** in the most relevant section
3. Follow this format:
   ```
   - **[Resource Name](URL)** — One or two sentences explaining what it is and why it's useful. *Optional note on who it's best for.*
   ```
4. **Open a Pull Request** with a clear title (e.g. `Add: Fast.ai Practical Deep Learning`)

---

## Style guide

- Keep comments concise: 1–2 sentences max per resource
- Use sentence case for descriptions (not Title Case)
- For papers, include the year and link to ArXiv or ACL Anthology when available
- Prefer the original source over aggregators (official course page > third-party recap)

---

## Reporting issues

Found a broken link or outdated resource? Open an issue with the title `[Fix] Resource name` and we'll handle it.
